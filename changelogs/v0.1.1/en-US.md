# SF6S Changelog - v0.1.1

## Added

- **Feedback Command**: Send questions, ideas, or suggestions directly to the bot without filling out a form.
  - Usage: `feedback your question or suggestion`
  - Example: `feedback Please add more move aliases`
  - Available on both the official QQ bot and OneBot. Mention the bot first in group chats, or send a private message.
- **Feedback in the QQ Command Menu**: Mention the bot in a group, select `反馈` from the command menu, add your feedback, and send.
