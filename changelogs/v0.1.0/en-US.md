# SF6S Changelog - v0.1.0

## Added

- **Official QQ Bot Support**: SF6S is now available through the official QQ bot platform.
- **Stats by Rank (`段位环境`)**: Compare character usage and win rates within a selected rank to understand the competition as you climb.
  - 🙌 Special thanks to QQ users 焕冥 and 悠哈 for their suggestions.
- **Stats by Character (`角色环境`)**: Compare a selected character's usage and win rates across ranks to help you choose a character.
  - 🙌 Special thanks to QQ users 焕冥 and 悠哈 for their suggestions.

## Improved

- **More Reliable Victory Announcements and Setback Notices**: Improved delivery logic to reduce missed notifications.
