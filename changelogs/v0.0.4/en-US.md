# SF6S Changelog - v0.0.4

## Added

- **Low HP Comeback Timestamps in Comments**: Video comments now feature automatic timestamp prompts for low HP comebacks (clutch reversals), allowing viewers to quickly locate and watch high-stakes highlight moments in matches.
- **Quick Replay Data Analysis Command**: Provides a brand-new match analysis command to swiftly parse submitted replay data and produce multi-dimensional statistics, helping players accurately review and analyze match details and performance.
