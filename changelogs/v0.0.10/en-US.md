# SF6S Changelog - v0.0.10

## Added

- **Player Lookup (`查询`)**: Look up a player's CFN ID by entering their username.
- **Character Usage Stats (`热度`)**: View character usage rates from the official website to see which characters are more popular among players.
  - 🙌 Special thanks to QQ users 焕冥 and 悠哈 for their suggestions.
- **Matchup Stats (`对阵`)**: View character matchup win rates from the official website to understand how each character performs overall in different matchups.
  - 🙌 Special thanks to QQ users 焕冥, 悠哈, and www.TH.zz for their suggestions.
- **Personal Win Rates (`胜率`)**: View a player's win rates with a chosen character against each opposing character to understand their individual matchup performance.

## Improved

- **Improved Interactions**: Updated feature guidance and query result presentation to avoid unhelpful replies.
  - 🙌 Special thanks to QQ users aaa 乐, 焕冥, and Hardman for their suggestions.
- **More Reliable Message Delivery**: Adjusted the message delivery strategy to reduce the likelihood of triggering platform risk controls and improve delivery reliability.
  - 🙌 Special thanks to QQ user Hardman for the suggestions and for sharing their experience.
